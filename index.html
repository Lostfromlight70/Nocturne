Nocturne

Some things are easier to say when the world gets quiet.

Take your time. There’s no rush.

01 · The surface

Calm on the outside.
A lot going on underneath.

I can look pretty calm when we’re together. In my head, I’m overthinking every sentence before it makes it out so I become silent a lot.

02 · The beginning

It started at the gym.

I started going on September 6, 2026. I met you on my fifth day there, and you caught my attention right away.

That part was immediate. Getting to know you made it grow.

03 · The little things

Then I got to know you.

Your laugh is really cute. You talk so sweetly, and you’re genuinely kind. Somehow, even when you’re nagging me, I end up liking you more.

04 · A small confession before the big one

There was that one time…

You were doing core exercises at the gym, and I was staring at you so much that you playfully told me to stop looking at you like that.

That’s when I realised just how obvious my staring had become and how little I see others in the room compared to you.

05 · It grew

I already liked you.
Then we kept talking.

I even spent time learning more about sportbikes than I did before because I felt a bit left out in the conversation and I wanted to understand more of what you like.

I just wanted more things we could talk about.

06 · Beneath the calm

I think more than I say.

I overthink what to say, how to say it, and whether I’ll sound stupid. Sometimes I hold back and say less than I wanted to.

This is me trying to say it without getting stuck in my own head.

07 · The risk

You call me your gym bestie.

I really value that. I’ve worried that being honest could change something good between us — but keeping this to myself didn’t feel right either.

For me, telling you the truth is worth the risk.

08 · The honest part

Mimi, I have feelings for you.

They were there from the beginning, and they grew stronger the more we talked. I value what we have, and I hope you feel something too — but I’ll respect whatever you feel.

You don’t owe me a particular answer. I just wanted you to know.
