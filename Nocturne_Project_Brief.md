# Nocturne --- My Personal Context and Website Brief

> This document is written from my point of view. It contains the
> personal context, story, feelings, and instructions I want used when
> building Nocturne. Treat the first-person sections as my account of
> what happened and how I feel. Do not change my story into invented
> events or feelings.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 1. What Nocturne Is

I want to make a website called **Nocturne** to tell Mimi that I have
feelings for her.

I don't want it to look like a generic confession website or an obvious
romantic page. I want the name and the opening to be mysterious, because
I want her to discover gradually that the website is about her. I chose
the name because it fits the atmosphere best; it doesn't need to have a
deeper personal meaning.

I want the website to feel cinematic, personal, a little playful, and
eventually honest and vulnerable. I want the beginning to be mysterious,
the middle to show my personality and our memories, and the end to be
direct about how I feel.

I'm planning to send her the website link through Snapchat, so it needs
to work especially well on a phone.

My intended setup is to keep the code on GitHub, connect the repository
to Netlify for hosting, and use Formspree for her response. I understand
that if Netlify is hosting the connected GitHub repository, GitHub is
where I store the code and Netlify serves the live website. I don't need
a separate GitHub Pages deployment unless I specifically decide I want
one too.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 2. How I Met Mimi

I started going to the gym on September 6, 2026. I met Mimi on my fifth
day there.

I found her attractive immediately. It wasn't something that slowly
developed from not being interested to suddenly finding her attractive.
I already liked how she looked from the beginning.

But the more we talked, the stronger my feelings became. Getting to know
her made me like her more than I already did.

She calls me her **"gym bestie."** I value that relationship, and it's
part of why I've been nervous about telling her how I feel. I don't want
to ruin something good between us, but I also feel that my feelings are
worth the risk of being honest.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 3. The Moment I Realised Just How Much I Liked Her

One moment that stands out to me happened when Mimi was doing core
exercises at the gym.

We were already talking a lot, and I was staring at her so much that she
playfully told me to stop looking at her like that. That's when I
realised just how much I had been staring.

I already knew I liked her before that happened. The moment didn't
create my feelings; it made me realise even more how obvious they had
become to me.

I want this memory included in Nocturne because it's personal to us and
has a bit of humour in it. I don't want it exaggerated or turned into a
dramatic romantic scene. It should feel like a real, slightly
embarrassing moment that I remember fondly.

Do not invent her facial expression, exact movements, extra dialogue, or
anything else that I haven't described.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 4. The Little Things I Like About Her

Her laugh is really cute. She talks so sweetly, and she's genuinely
kind.

Even when she's nagging me, I can't help but fall for her even more. I
like these little things about her, not just her appearance.

I want Nocturne to show that my feelings have grown through getting to
know her. I don't want it to sound like a list of compliments copied
from a romance website. These details should appear naturally as part of
the story.

Mimi likes pink, sportbikes, and cars. I like cars and bikes too, but I
didn't know much about sportbikes, so I spent time learning about
them---things like engines, horsepower, RPM, and different models---so I
would have things to talk about with her.

That doesn't mean I think she owes me anything for making the effort. I
just wanted to understand an interest of hers and have more things we
could talk about.

Mimi also runs a business selling luxury items and curated gifts,
including matching or couple items, money bouquets, and gifts or plans
for couples. This detail is optional for the website; the confession
should remain about my feelings and not turn into an advertisement for
her business.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 5. What Happens Inside My Head Around Her

I'm not as calm as I seem on the inside each time I see her.

I can look calm around her, but I overthink things. I think about what
to say, how to say it, and whether I'll sound stupid. Sometimes I end up
holding back or saying less than I wanted to because I'm thinking too
much.

I want Nocturne to communicate that contrast between how I appear and
how I actually feel. Mimi might not realise how much is going on in my
head when we're together.

I don't want to be portrayed as helpless or as if she controls my entire
life. I just want her to understand that I'm more nervous and affected
by being around her than I usually show.

The website is a way for me to express what I have struggled to say
face-to-face.

------------------------------------------------------------------------

# 6. Why I'm Confessing

I've had feelings for Mimi, and they became stronger the more we talked.

Because she calls me her gym bestie, I've been worried about ruining a
really good relationship. I value what we have, and I don't want to lose
the friendship just because I've admitted that I feel something more.

But for me, it's worth the risk.

I want to be honest with her instead of continuing to keep these
feelings to myself. I'm not confessing because I think she owes me a
chance, and I'm not assuming that she feels the same way. I just want
her to know the truth about how I feel.

I hope she feels something too, but I'll respect whatever she feels. I
genuinely hope we can still be friends no matter the answer.

This is the heart of Nocturne:

> I've had feelings for you, and they became stronger the more we
> talked. I value what we have, and because you call me your gym bestie,
> I was worried about ruining a really good relationship. But for me,
> it's worth the risk to be honest. I hope you feel something too, but
> I'll respect whatever you feel.

This paragraph is my plain-language explanation of the confession. The
final website copy can be polished, but it must keep this meaning and
should still sound like me.

# 7. What I Want Mimi to Experience

I chose a **hybrid format**: a cinematic opening followed by interactive
chapters and a final confession screen.

I want the website to start mysterious and gradually reveal that it is
about her. I don't want the first screen to say something obvious like
"I have feelings for you." I want her to become curious, then start
recognising the details, and finally understand what I've been trying to
tell her.

The overall progression should be:

1.  **Curiosity:** She opens Nocturne and sees something atmospheric and
    intriguing.
2.  **The surface:** The story introduces the idea that someone can look
    calm while having a lot going on internally.
3.  **Recognition:** Details about the gym and our conversations begin
    to make the story feel personal.
4.  **Playfulness:** The story brings in things like her laugh, her
    kindness, and the core-exercise staring incident.
5.  **Vulnerability:** I explain that my feelings grew and that I have
    been nervous about risking our friendship.
6.  **The confession:** I tell her directly that I have feelings for
    her.
7.  **Her space:** She gets an open text box where she can write
    anything she wants.
8.  **A gentle ending:** I tell her I hope she feels something too, but
    I respect her answer and hope we can remain friends.

I want a mixture of mystery, playful personality, and sincerity. It
should feel like a small story, not a long letter pasted onto a webpage.

# 8. How I Want the Website to Sound

I want the writing to have three qualities:

-   **Mysterious and cinematic** at the beginning.
-   **Playful and a little quirky** in the middle.
-   **Sincere, vulnerable, and direct** at the end.

I don't want every line to sound like poetry. I want some natural humour
and a few personal details that make it feel like me. When the
confession comes, I want it to be clear and honest rather than hidden
behind metaphors.

The website should not sound like a generic romance template. It should
not make up events, conversations, or feelings that I haven't described.
It should not claim that Mimi likes me back or suggest that her being
friendly proves anything romantic.

I want it to communicate my feelings without making her responsible for
my happiness or pressuring her to reciprocate.

# 9. Visual Style

I want Nocturne to have a dark, mysterious, cinematic aesthetic.

## Colours

Use this palette as a starting point:

-   Near-black: `#080A10`
-   Deep midnight blue: `#0D1424`
-   Secondary blue: `#17243A`
-   Muted pink accent: `#D88DA8`
-   Soft pale text: `#F2F0F3`
-   Muted secondary text: `#A6A9B5`
-   Subtle border: `rgba(242, 240, 243, 0.14)`

I want pink to be a restrained accent, partly because Mimi likes pink. I
don't want the entire website to be pink.

## Typography

Use an elegant serif font for headings and important lines, with a clean
sans-serif font for body text, buttons, and forms. The text must remain
easy to read on mobile.

## Visual motifs

I like the idea of subtle shadows, blurred lights, midnight gradients,
light trails, and restrained automotive-inspired details. The car/bike
influence should be atmospheric rather than making the website look like
a dealership or racing game.

I don't want random stock photos of couples. The site should work well
with abstract visuals and typography alone. Do not use photos of Mimi or
other people unless I provide and approve them.

## Animation

Use subtle fades, smooth transitions, and small interactive details.
Keep it polished, but don't let animations slow the site down or prevent
someone from reading. Respect reduced-motion preferences.

This is the intended content plan. The exact wording can be developed
during implementation, but the facts and meaning must remain accurate.

Show the name **Nocturne**, a mysterious line, and one clear button to

roses, and generic romantic symbols.

Introduce the idea that a calm appearance doesn't always reveal what's

## Chapter Three --- The little things

Bring in some of the details I like about her: her cute laugh, sweet way

These details should feel natural, not like a checklist.

Tell the story of Mimi doing core exercises while we were already
talking a lot. I was staring at her so much that she playfully told me
to stop looking at her like that. That was when I realised just how much
I had been staring.

Keep this section playful and true to what I said. Don't invent
additional dialogue or gestures.

## Chapter Five --- How the feelings grew

Explain that I already liked her, but the more we talked, the stronger

## Chapter Six --- Beneath the calm

Show that I may look calm when I'm around her, but I overthink what to

## Chapter Seven --- The risk

Explain that she calls me her gym bestie, that I value our friendship,

## The confession

State plainly that I have feelings for her, that those feelings grew
through our conversations, that I value our friendship, and that I hope

feels.

## Her response

Give her one completely open text box. Do not provide multiple-choice
answers, sample responses, or prompts pushing her toward a particular
answer. It must be optional.

A simple neutral label could be:

> "If you want to say anything, you can leave it here."

This is a wording direction, not a required final sentence.

## Ending

Keep it short and gentle. Let her know she doesn't need to answer
immediately, that I hope she feels something too, and that I genuinely
hope we can remain friends regardless of her answer.

# 11. The Response Form

I want Mimi to have an unrestricted place to type anything that's on her
mind.

I do **not** want: - Multiple-choice feelings. - Suggested replies. -
Buttons such as "I like you too" or "Let's be friends." - Leading
questions that push her toward a particular answer. - A requirement to
explain or justify her feelings. - Unnecessary requests for her name,
phone number, email, or other personal information.

The response form will use Formspree. The actual Formspree endpoint and
destination inbox need to be configured before launch; do not invent an
endpoint.

The form must: - Be optional. - Clearly indicate when a submission is
being sent. - Show a confirmation only after a successful submission. -
Show an honest error if the submission fails. - Preserve the text she
entered if submission fails, so she can retry. - Keep her response
private rather than displaying it publicly on the website.

I should know which inbox receives her message and protect access to it.
Do not claim that any third-party form can guarantee absolute
confidentiality.

# 12. Technical Plan

Keep the project simple and beginner-friendly. Use separate HTML, CSS,
and JavaScript files. Avoid unnecessary frameworks unless there is a
clear reason to use one.

Suggested folder structure:

``` text
nocturne/
├── index.html
├── style.css
├── script.js
├── assets/
│   └── (optional images, icons, and backgrounds)
├── README.md
└── .gitignore
```

### `index.html`

Contains the semantic structure, chapters, navigation controls,
confession, response form, and suitable page metadata.

### `style.css`

Contains the colour palette, typography, responsive layouts, chapter
transitions, button styles, form states, focus indicators, and
reduced-motion support.

### `script.js`

Controls chapter navigation, reveal interactions, button behaviour, and
the Formspree submission states.

Keep the code organised and easy for me to understand and update.
